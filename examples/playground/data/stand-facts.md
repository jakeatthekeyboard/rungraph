# Sunny's Lemonade Stand: the facts

- **Owner:** Sunny, age 11
- **Where:** corner of Oak Street and 5th Avenue
- **When:** Saturdays, 10am to 4pm
- **Price:** $2 a cup, $5 for three cups
- **Made with:** fresh-squeezed lemons, cane sugar, filtered water
- **Flavours:** classic, strawberry (new this summer)
- **Extra:** 10% of every sale goes to the local animal shelter
